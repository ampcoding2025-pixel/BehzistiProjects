AI Support & Ticketing Bot — Vendor Scorecard

Fill during / after the interview. Score each category 1–5. Weighted total = sum((score÷5)×weight) for scored categories, normalized to 100.

Scoring guide: 5 Exceeds · 4 Strong · 3 Acceptable · 2 Weak · 1 Poor / does not meet
Decision bands: 80+ Strong candidate · 65–79 Conditional · 50–64 Weak · <50 Not recommended · Any unanswered Critical = Hold
