AI Support & Ticketing Bot — Interview Checklist

Ask each question, tick when answered, jot short notes. Transfer category scores to the scorecard.

1. AI & product capabilities — Weight 15%

✓PriorityQuestionNotes
CRITICALWhat LLM / models power the bot? Can we choose or bring our own?
